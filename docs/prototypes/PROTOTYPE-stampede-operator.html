Everything here is illustrative. No provider discovery, model calls, credentials, file changes, or deployment occur. Reloading discards the session. Simulated grants and readiness checks confer no real authority.

Setup first; launch separately

Setup prepares and saves the team and draft brief. It ends without starting delivery workers. Launch is a separate action that rechecks readiness and the agreed limits. Requested kickoff research is separately bounded.

Pilot horizon: 4 hours total. A later break cannot extend that horizon. Time buttons simulate events; no clock is running.

Production and service registration remain stop boundaries in this example. Credential handling requires an outside human session.

Handoffs and decisions

    What you would inspect

    Team: recommended planner, generalist workers, independent reviewer, and one contract-lawyer; workers start for useful work. A research kickoff uses a small provisional team before the final roster is agreed.

    Usage: per-model token velocity, available quota, worker time, tool waits, and mediation cost. This demo has no measurements; unknown usage stays unknown.

    Herdr: terminal workspace, panes, agent processes, and their controls.

    Stampede: goal, configuration, assignments, stop policy, evidence, checkpoints, and decision history. Enforcement stays outside model judgment.
